Decentralized Digital Democracy for Carbon Accountability

Implement a blockchain-based platform where individuals, businesses, and governments can track, trade, and verify carbon footprints in real time. Smart contracts automate carbon credit exchanges, ensuring transparency, while decentralized governance empowers communities to set and enforce emission targets. Incentivize low-carbon actions with tokenized rewards, fostering collective accountability. (Summary: A decentralized digital system for transparent, democratic carbon tracking and trading to accelerate emissions reduction.)
